# Customer Complaint Handling Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how customer complaints are received, logged, escalated, and resolved, so that every customer gets a consistent and timely response regardless of which channel they use to reach us.

## Intake Channels

Customers may submit complaints by email, phone, or live chat. Every complaint, regardless of channel, must be logged in the customer relationship management (CRM) system within 24 hours of receipt, including the customer's account details, a description of the issue, and the channel through which it was received.

## Severity Tiers

Complaints are triaged into three tiers upon logging. Tier 1 (critical) covers issues such as billing errors, data privacy concerns, or complete service outages affecting a customer's ability to use the product; these require an initial response within 4 hours. Tier 2 (standard) covers functional bugs, feature complaints, or service quality concerns; these require an initial response within 24 hours. Tier 3 (low priority) covers general feedback, feature requests, or minor inconveniences; these require an initial response within 3 business days.

## Escalation

If a Tier 1 complaint is not resolved within 24 hours of the initial response, it is automatically escalated to a team lead. Any complaint involving a threat of legal action, media attention, or a request to cancel an enterprise contract is escalated immediately to the customer success director, regardless of tier.

## Resolution and Follow-Up

A complaint is not considered closed until the customer has confirmed the issue is resolved or has not responded to two follow-up attempts over five business days. All resolved complaints are reviewed weekly by the customer success team to identify recurring issues that may indicate a broader product or process problem.

## Documentation

Every interaction related to a complaint, including internal notes and customer communications, must be recorded in the CRM so that any team member can pick up the case with full context if the original handler is unavailable.
