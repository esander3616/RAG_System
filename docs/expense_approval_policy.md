# Expense Approval Policy

## Purpose

This policy governs how employees submit business expenses for reimbursement and defines the approval required at different spending levels.

## Covered Categories

Reimbursable expenses fall into five categories: travel, software and subscriptions, client entertainment, office equipment, and training and conferences. Expenses outside these categories require pre-approval from a manager before the purchase is made, not just before reimbursement.

## Submission

Employees must submit expense requests within 30 days of the purchase date, along with an itemized receipt. Requests submitted after 30 days may be denied at the discretion of finance unless there is a documented reason for the delay.

## Approval Threshold

Any single expense request of $500 or less may be automatically approved by the finance system without additional review, provided it falls within one of the covered categories and includes a valid receipt. Any single expense request exceeding $500 requires manager sign-off before reimbursement is processed. The employee's direct manager must review and approve the request in the expense system; finance will not process a reimbursement above this threshold without that approval on file.

## Reimbursement Timeline

Approved expenses are reimbursed on the next standard payroll cycle following approval. Expenses awaiting manager sign-off are not considered approved and will not be included in that cycle's reimbursement batch.

## Prohibited Expenses

The company does not reimburse personal entertainment unrelated to client business, traffic or parking fines, or upgrades to travel class beyond what is specified in the travel policy. Any request containing a prohibited expense will be returned to the employee for correction rather than partially approved.
