# Information Security Policy

## Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the minimum security standards for protecting company systems, data, and customer information. It applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties with access to company systems, regardless of location or device.

## Data Classification

All company data falls into one of four categories: Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Public data may be shared freely outside the company. Internal data is intended for employee use only and should not be shared externally without approval. Confidential data includes customer records, financial data, and employee personal information, and requires encryption at rest and in transit. Restricted data includes authentication credentials, encryption keys, and legal records, and may only be accessed by employees with an explicit business need, subject to manager approval.

## Access Control

Access to company systems follows the principle of least privilege: employees are granted only the access required to perform their job function. Multi-factor authentication is required for all systems that store or process Confidential or Restricted data. Access reviews are conducted quarterly, and any account inactive for more than 90 days is automatically disabled pending manager review.

## Password Requirements

All company account passwords must be at least 12 characters long and contain a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Passwords must be rotated every 90 days and may not reuse any of the previous five passwords. Passwords must never be shared, written down in an unsecured location, or reused across personal and company accounts.

## Incident Response

Any suspected security incident, including lost devices, phishing attempts, or unauthorized access, must be reported to the security team within one hour of discovery. The security team will triage all reports within four hours and will notify affected employees or customers within 72 hours if an incident is confirmed to involve a data breach, consistent with applicable regulatory requirements.

## Device Security

All company-issued laptops and mobile devices must have full-disk encryption enabled and must be enrolled in the company's mobile device management system before connecting to internal systems. Personal devices used to access company email or documents must have a passcode enabled and must support remote wipe.

## Acceptable Use

Company systems are provided for business purposes. Employees should have no expectation of privacy when using company systems, and all activity may be logged and reviewed for security and compliance purposes. Installing unapproved software on company devices requires prior approval from IT.
